# Cashu Ecosystem Roadmap
### 2025 Look Back → 2026 Forward

---

## Executive Summary

**2025 turned Cashu from a protocol into an ecosystem**

- Shift from **spec-first** → **production-ready tooling**
- Multiple stable wallets and SDKs in use
- Real-world usage across payments, AI, communities, offline systems
- High experimentation, but **durable core infrastructure**

**Key takeaway:**
Cashu is becoming a **general-purpose, private payment layer** for Bitcoin-native apps.

---

## 2025 Look Back: Protocol & Standards

### What Stabilized
- Core NUTs matured:
  - Auth (NUT-21/22)
  - SIG_ALL / P2PK / HTLC
  - Mint info, fees, keysets
  - HTTP 402
  - BOLT12 + multi-mint flows
- Fewer breaking changes, more refinement

### Why It Matters
- Wallets and apps can build with confidence
- Protocol churn is no longer the dominant risk

**Status:** Foundational layer is solid and stewarded

---

## 2025 Look Back: Core Infrastructure

### Libraries & Tooling
- CDK → production-grade, multi-language
- cashu-ts → v3.x adoption
- Swift / Kotlin / Python / C# bindings
- Nutshell + mint ops tooling
- Key rotation, fees, remote signing, deterministic derivations

### Shift Observed
- From “*Can I build this?*”
- To “*How should I build this?*”

**Status:** Infrastructure is ready for serious developers

---

## 2025 Look Back: Wallet Layer

### Stable Wallets
- cashu.me
- Macadamia
- Minibits
- Sovran
- Zeus integration

### Key Improvements
- Seed-based recovery
- Mint discovery & ratings
- NFC & offline support
- Multinut handling
- Improved UX for lockups and P2PK

**Status:** Wallets are now products, not demos

---

## 2025 Look Back: Applications & Use Cases

### What Worked
- AI micropayments (CypherFlow, Routstr)
- Community payments (Cashu for Communities)
- Mining pools (Hashpool)
- Offline & protest tooling (Bitchat)
- HTTP 402 demos → production readiness
- Hackathons as idea filters

### What Didn’t
- Single-purpose novelty apps
- Demos without users

**Status:** Healthy experimentation funnel

---

## What We Learned in 2025

### Key Observations
- Projects are disposable — **maintainers are not**
- Longevity follows dependency
- Ecosystem is no longer fragile
- Cashu’s strength is **composability**

---

## End of 2025: Ecosystem Snapshot

### We Have
- Stable protocol
- Production SDKs
- Multiple maintained wallets
- Live mints
- Real users

### We Don’t Yet Have
- Clear mint trust standards
- Strong wallet interoperability guarantees
- Obvious default onboarding paths
- Sustainable maintainer funding loops

---

## Looking Forward: 2026 Focus Areas

---

## 2026 Focus: Protocol Direction

### Priorities
- Finalize edge cases (SIG_ALL, P2PK, HTLC)
- Clarify mint behavior expectations
- Reduce ambiguity in NUTs
- Fewer new NUTs, more convergence

**Goal:**
Make Cashu *boring* to implement correctly

---

## 2026 Focus: Wallet Interoperability & UX

### Open Questions
- Backup & restore across wallets
- Safe mint switching
- Communicating trust and risk

### Likely Priorities
- Mint lists & discovery standards
- Wallet-connect-style flows
- Clear user-facing risk signals

---

## 2026 Focus: Mint Ecosystem Health

### Challenges
- Trust without KYC
- Operator best practices
- Fee transparency
- Reputation without centralization

### Direction
- Social trust (Web of Trust)
- Deterministic behavior
- Clear failure modes

---

## 2026 Focus: High-Leverage Applications

### Encourage
- HTTP 402 / pay-per-request
- AI & compute markets
- Community governance & funding
- Offline & constrained environments
- Tooling that hides complexity

### Deprioritize
- Novelty wallets
- One-off demos without users

---

## Strategic Question for the Ecosystem

**Not:**
“Can Cashu work?”

**But:**
“What kind of ecosystem do we want Cashu to enable?”

- Payments for the internet
- Privacy layer for Bitcoin apps
- Coordination tool for communities
- Composable money primitive

---

## Final Takeaway

- Cashu crossed the **fragile phase** in 2025
- The ecosystem can now afford to:
  - Kill bad ideas
  - Say no
  - Focus on depth over breadth

**This is maturity, not stagnation.**
